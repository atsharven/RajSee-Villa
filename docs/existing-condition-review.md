# Existing villa and photo review

14 November 2026 · Ansal A-60, Ajmer

The source folder contains a one-page plan and 167 image files. Of the images, 164 are site photographs, including repeated views. One is an image of the drawing and two are renovation concept renders. I used the original drawing to locate partitions and read its written dimensions, then checked the visible surfaces and openings against the site photos. The model uses the November front-yard condition. ## Dimensions to confirm on site

- The drawing records a 14.50 × 29.00 m site and 420.50 m². Its red outline and its room dimensions use inconsistent proportions. The model retains the written site size as metadata, and shows roughly 5.1 m at the front and 2 m at the back for the photographed enclosure. The location of the remaining plot depth is unresolved.
- Floor surfaces are at 0, 3.30 and 6.46 m in the model. The number and rise of the steps, intermediate landings, clearances and exact floor-to-floor heights need measurement. The small-room footprint on the upper floor is provisionally represented as part of the landing. The earlier owner account describes the roof tank above the stair-head room. Its dimensions and supports are estimates.
- The large openings, steps, wall offsets and terraces can be used to discuss circulation and layout. Door widths, wall thicknesses, heights, finishes and cabinetry are approximate. The neighbour's hidden elevations and the structural role of each wall remain unverified.

The original source files remain in the supplied photo folder. The clean timber-and-grey frontage in the two concept images belongs to an earlier design exercise.
