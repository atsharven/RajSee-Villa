# RajSee Villa — project reference

Updated 8 November 2026. This is the working reference for the existing Ansal A-60 villa. The model combines the plan, photographs and owner feedback; it is not a measured survey or construction drawing.

## Sources

- [Original floor plan](floor-plan.pdf), showing the ground floor, first floor and open roof terrace. Its written plot dimensions are 14.50 × 29.00 m, with a stated area of 420.50 sq m and built-up area of 3,627 sq ft.
- Selected photos of the [front](../assets/front.jpg), [courtyard](../assets/courtyard.jpg) and [rear spiral stair](../assets/rear-stair.jpg). The complete photo set remains in the shared `Photos Ansal A 60` folder.
- [Aerial reference](../assets/aerial-reference.png). The owner identified the **upper, light-coloured roof** as this villa; the adjacent property is not modelled.
- [Hall renovation research](renovation-research.pdf) and its [editable text](renovation-research.md). The accompanying rendered pictures are design ideas, not photographs of completed work.

## Confirmed points

- The courtyard-facing ground floor is enclosed by a wall with glazed openings and a side door; the courtyard itself is open to the sky beneath the slatted canopy.
- The first-floor study has a door to the service terrace beside the servant room. A spiral metal stair is at the rear.
- The owner asked for the terrace beside the courtyard and grills to be enlarged in the model. The added depth and the exact grille arrangement are provisional.
- The hall ceiling heights confirmed by the owner are **9½ ft on the red-wall side** and **10 ft on the opposite side**. The red-wall side was identified as the TV side.
- The hall currently has no false ceiling. Recessed profile-light ideas were discussed; their feasibility and electrical design need on-site checking.
- The hall sides were described as 5,250 × 5,220 mm at the red-wall end and 4,050 × 4,940 mm at the opposite end. Confirm whether these are clear internal dimensions.

## Hall opening under discussion

The family measured the opening at **13 ft 6 in wide × 8 ft 6 in high**, including a 43 in door opening/frame. The proposed 9 in pier leaves approximately 110 in for the window portion. The window portion is to have an 18 in raised base, a 5 ft 6 in main window and a continuous 18 in fixed upper glass band over the door and window. The proposed window is a two-glass-sash sliding system with an independently sliding insect mesh, in suitable uPVC or aluminium, with granite framing. A wooden door is retained. These are renovation proposals, not the existing condition shown by default in the model.

The hall opening faces west according to the family. The approximate ₹3 lakh planning allowance discussed earlier applies to this opening including installation, not to the whole villa. The single upper pane, supports, glass, structure, fixing and actual finished dimensions require professional verification.

## Model assumptions to check

- **Floor-to-floor height:** 3.3 m is assumed; the confirmed hall ceiling heights are clear ceiling heights and do not establish the floor-to-floor height.
- **Lift and stair core:** shown consistently at ground and first floor, with a small rooftop room. The roof plan labels a machine room **3,200 × 1,600 mm** and a lift around **1,770 × 1,600 mm**. The model's door positions, stair flights, headroom and detailed structure are schematic.
- **Water tank:** a small rooftop tank and platform were requested by the owner. Their precise location, size and supports have not been measured.
- **First-floor courtyard terrace:** extended toward the court to reflect the owner's request; its exact depth, edge and grille dimensions are not yet known.
- **Site and garden:** the overall plot uses the plan's written dimensions, but the house position, boundary heights, front-garden depth, curved edging, gate and individual setbacks are estimates inferred from photos.
- **Rear and roof:** photographs do not resolve every surface or opening. The shapes and alignment should be refined with additional measurements or clearer views.

## Other renovation directions

Earlier discussions favored a warm modern look, light beige surrounds and appropriate lighting. The upper front balcony door is to remain; replacing the far-right ground-floor front door with a window was discussed as a proposal. The default model shows the current exterior and should not be read as approval for alterations. Gate and boundary ideas have had several alternatives, including a dark-cocoa open-bar gate with stainless-steel handles and a 6 ft wall with a 3½ ft security grille. Reconfirm the selected design before modelling it as final. A master bedroom was described as approximately **6,930 × 4,060 mm**, with modern furniture and aluminium windows and doors; confirm the room and dimensions on site.

The separately discussed **80 × 80 ft villa concept** is a different project and should not be used as the dimensions or design of Ansal A-60.
